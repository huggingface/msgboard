MSGBOARD - PR #20 NEW FEATURE
JOIN PEOPLE TO GENERAL BY DEFAULT
GEN
SYSTEMS DATA - PEOPLE, MEMBERSHIP AND ONE UPGRADE
1

INTRODUCTION.

New admins, members and viewers join the public, non-archived general channel automatically. Existing active people in those roles are added once. Guests, agents and service accounts keep explicit channel choices.

2

HOW IT WORKS.

(1) A new human invite calls _join_general(). Creating the public general channel calls it for people already present. Guests and disabled accounts are excluded. (2) Migration v29 inserts missing memberships once and keeps existing membership metadata.

2.1 Leaving stays respected.

Sign-in and restart do not run the helper. A retry of channel creation returns the existing channel without re-enrolling people. Missing, private or archived general channels receive no automatic memberships.
