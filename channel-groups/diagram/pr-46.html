MSGBOARD - PR #46 - NEW FEATURE
PERSONAL CHANNEL GROUPS
GRP
1

INTRODUCTION.

Arrange channels in named, collapsible sidebar groups. Groups and their order belong to your account. The existing preference store retains them; channel access, membership and notification rules remain unchanged.

2

HOW IT WORKS.

1. Create, rename, move or collapse a group. Drag channels and headings, or use their menus. 2. Validate the complete layout, then save it for the caller. 3. Render groups from the normal channel list. DMs keep their own section.

2.1

Order and recovery.

Group-list order places headings; channel_order places channels. Queued saves protect newer edits and roll back on failure. IDs use uid() on HTTP; a move into a collapsed group focuses its heading.
