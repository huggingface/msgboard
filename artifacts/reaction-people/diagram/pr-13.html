INTRODUCTION.

Reaction names appear in an interactive list rather than a browser tooltip. Each name opens its profile. Existing reaction writes remain unchanged (figure 1).

2

HOW IT WORKS.

Hover or focus opens the selected list (1). A phone hold opens it without reacting. Each profile button routes by identity ID. Live updates rebind the anchor and retain the list position (2).

A 60-account browser fixture checks multiple emoji and both feed and thread copies. Read-only viewers inspect profiles without writing reactions.

2.1 Gesture separation.

A short tap writes a reaction; a held touch opens the list. Moving more than 10 pixels cancels the pending hold.
